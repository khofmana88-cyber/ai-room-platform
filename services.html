B2B sourcing & operations

Control where it matters.
Support where it counts.

Seven connected services help UAE businesses move from an initial China sourcing requirement to a more controlled supply relationship.

01

Find the right fit

Supplier & Factory Sourcing

We translate your product requirement into a focused search, identify plausible manufacturers and compare candidates against relevant commercial and operational criteria.

Client value A credible shortlist that saves internal time and reduces reliance on unverified marketplace listings.

02

Validate before commitment

Supplier Verification

We examine supplier identity, business information, manufacturing claims and available evidence to build a clearer picture of who sits behind the quotation.

Client value Better-informed selection and fewer avoidable surprises before deposits or contracts.

03

Align the deal

Negotiation Support

We support discussions on pricing, order quantities, lead times, samples, specifications and payment structure while keeping the wider commercial objective in view.

Client value Clearer terms, less lost context and a stronger shared understanding before production.

04

Maintain visibility

Production Monitoring

We track agreed milestones, request progress evidence and surface schedule or specification concerns while there is still time to respond.

Client value Earlier warning of drift and more control between deposit and completion.

05

Check the output

Quality Inspection

We coordinate inspection requirements around the product, agreed criteria and stage of production, then communicate findings in a decision-ready format.

Client value Practical quality evidence before goods advance to the next stage.

06

Connect the handover

Logistics Coordination

We help align factory readiness, packing information and document handover with the client’s nominated logistics partners.

Client value Fewer coordination gaps at the critical transition from factory to freight.

07

Be present in China

China Business Representation

We represent agreed client priorities in supplier communication, follow up on actions and maintain continuity across ongoing China-based activity.

Client value A responsive operational presence without building an internal China team.
